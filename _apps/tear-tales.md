faq:
  - q: What do I need to run Tear Tales?
    a: An iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later. To sync your records between devices, sign in to iCloud on each of them.
  - q: Who can read my records?
    a: Only you. Your records are stored on your device and in your own iCloud account; I have no server and cannot see them. Lock a record and turn on Private Mode to hide its note from anyone looking over your shoulder.
  - q: How does Private Mode work?
    a: Mark a record as locked with the lock button when you write it, then turn on Private Mode in Settings. The notes of locked records are hidden in the list, and opening or editing them asks for Face ID or Touch ID. Five locked records are free; Tear Tales Pro has no limit.
  - q: What does Tear Tales Pro add?
    a: "Tear Tales is free and shows a small sponsor banner. Tear Tales Pro, an in-app purchase, adds search, unlimited tags and locked records, import and export of your records, and removes the banner."
  - q: Can I export my records?
    a: Yes, with Tear Tales Pro. Under Import and Export in Settings you can save your records as a CSV table, a JSON file or plain text, and import them again from a CSV or JSON file in the same format.
  - q: Do you collect data about me?
    a: Tear Tales has no account. It uses usage analytics, which you can switch off in Settings, and crash reports to improve the app, and the free version shows a sponsor banner. The content of your records is never sent to me. The [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/) explains each service.
  - q: How do I get in touch?
    a: Write to [apps@t-schmutz.de](mailto:apps@t-schmutz.de), or use "Give feedback" in the app's Settings.
